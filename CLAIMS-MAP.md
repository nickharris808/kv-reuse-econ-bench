# CLAIMS-MAP — kv-reuse-econ-bench

**Tag: CLEAN. Licence: Apache-2.0.**

This file exists so the CLEAN tag is *auditable* rather than asserted.

## The line

Every method independent in the filed set terminates in a **physical actuation** step — provisioning
a resource, admitting a subject into an executing path, serving or refusing cached state, writing
bytes into a relying party's environment.

This benchmark **recomputes numbers from certificates and prints them.** It performs no terminal
step of any claim.

| Filed claim family | What it recites | What this benchmark does instead |
|---|---|---|
| the pooled reuse fabric and fleet controller | the apparatus that performs the mechanism and then **admits or refuses an operation upon a physical resource** | Recomputes a published accounting figure from a certificate and prints the distribution. It operates no fabric, routes nothing, and provisions nothing. |
| Conformance methodology | running a declared battery with controls and emitting a certificate that **gates** a deployment | Verifies figures against a bundled certificate and reports. The exit code is a reporting convention; nothing is gated. |

## What the bundled fixtures are

The certificates under `fixtures/` carry every **measured value** from our committed record, which
is what `--verify` recomputes. Where a certificate also carried an internal module path or the name
of a compiled artifact, that string is **redacted in the published copy** — it points at the
licensed implementation and at no number this tool checks. Redacted fixtures say so in a
`_PUBLISHED_COPY_NOTE` field.

## Non-claims

- A successful `--verify` attests that the published figure **follows from the published rows**. It
  attests nothing about whether those rows describe your system.
- These are our own measurements and support no claim about any third party.
